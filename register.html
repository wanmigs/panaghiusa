Register for 2026

Sunday 29 November, 5:00–8:00 PM at Pearlmont Hotel, Cagayan de Oro City. The yearly CDO MAS-AMiCUS Alumni gathering, for a cause.

How we use your details

The organizers collect your name, email, mobile number, city, how you heard about Panaghiusa and your payment reference, so they can plan the night and match your fee.

Answers are stored in Google Forms and a linked Google Sheet, shared only with the organizers. They are deleted or anonymized 12 months after the event. To have yours removed sooner, message the organizers.

This site has no analytics and sets no cookies.

  • Sunday, 29 November 2026
  • 5:00–8:00 PM, Philippine time
  • Pearlmont Hotel, Cagayan de Oro City

How it works

  1. Open the form. It takes about two minutes on a phone, and you don’t need a Google account.
  2. Send the registration fee to our GCash or GoTyme account from any bank or e-wallet you use. The amount and account details are in the form.
  3. Enter your payment reference number and submit.

The fee and the 2026 cause are listed at the top of the form.
