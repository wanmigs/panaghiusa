One night a year, for a cause

Panaghiusa means unity. Once a year, near the end of the year, the CDO MAS-AMiCUS Alumni come back together in Cagayan de Oro: batchmates, their families and their kids.

Every edition now raises something for a named beneficiary. Your registration covers your place on the night, and part of it goes to the cause.

In 2025 that was House of Hope Foundation in Lumbia: medicines, a hospital bed and cleaning materials, plus the renovation of a church comfort room.

We say what each edition gave, and we only print numbers the organizers can stand behind.
