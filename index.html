One Night a Year, United for a Cause

Panaghiusa means unity. What began in 2021 as a simple gathering of MAS-AMiCUS alumni in Cagayan de Oro has grown into an annual tradition of friendship, faith, and service, a time to reconnect, thank God for His faithfulness, and share His love with others.

Through the years, our collective efforts have supported school supplies donations, feeding programs, and community and church projects. In 2025, we extended assistance to the House of Hope Foundation in Lumbia and contributed to the renovation of SDA Samay Church’s comfort room.

This year, we welcome you to the 5th Panaghiusa with a Cause. A portion of the registration proceeds will support a church’s need for a Smart TV and provide recreational equipment and essential supplies for a rehabilitation center in Balingasag.

Register for this year’s gathering, explore our past projects, and join us as we continue serving together.

Together in faith. United for a cause.
